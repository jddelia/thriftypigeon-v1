# Article & Playbook Positioning Copy

## Core Positioning Strategy

### Free Articles = Complete Solutions
Articles solve the problem fully. Someone should be able to implement the advice without buying anything. No artificial cliffs or withheld information.

### Paid Playbooks = Implementation Accelerators  
Playbooks save setup time with ready-made tools. They're convenience, not necessity. They make implementation faster and easier, but never required for success.

## Article Positioning Templates

### Article Introduction Formulas

**Formula 1: Personal Story + System**
```
"Here's exactly how I [achieved specific result] using this [specific system]. 
I'll walk you through every step, including the mistakes I made and how to avoid them. 
[Optional: At the end, I'll share the exact spreadsheet/template I built.]"
```

**Formula 2: Problem + Complete Solution**
```
"If you're trying to [solve specific problem], this guide covers everything you need to know. 
You'll get the complete strategy, real examples with numbers, and step-by-step instructions. 
No prerequisites required—just follow along."
```

**Formula 3: Beginner-Friendly Promise**
```
"Whether this is your first time [doing X] or you've tried before without success, 
this guide breaks it down into simple steps that work with any [budget/income/situation]. 
Everything you need is right here."
```

### Article Value Statements

**For beginners:**
- "No experience needed"
- "Start with what you have"
- "Written in plain English"
- "Step-by-step with real examples"

**For everyone:**
- "Complete system in one place"
- "Everything you need to get started"
- "Real numbers, not just theory"
- "Honest about what works and what doesn't"

**For action-takers:**
- "Ready to implement today"
- "Skip the research phase"
- "All the details in one guide"
- "Template/spreadsheet included" (if applicable)

## Playbook Positioning Templates

### How to Position Playbooks (What They Are)

**Option 1: Time-Saver Frame**
```
"Want to skip the setup work? The [Article Name] Playbook includes:
✓ The exact spreadsheet (no need to build from scratch)
✓ Pre-written email templates
✓ Print-ready checklists
✓ Step-by-step implementation guide

Get the toolkit for $[X] and start implementing today."
```

**Option 2: Tool Collection Frame**
```
"Ready to put this into action? Get the complete toolkit:
✓ [Specific tool 1] (saves X hours of setup)
✓ [Specific tool 2] (with formulas already built)
✓ [Specific tool 3] (ready to print/use)
✓ Bonus: [Extra resource]

Everything you need for $[X]."
```

**Option 3: Convenience Frame**
```
"Love the strategy but want the tools ready-made? 
The playbook includes all the spreadsheets, templates, and checklists mentioned in this guide—built and ready to use for $[X]."
```

### What NOT to Say About Playbooks

❌ **Avoid:**
- "Advanced strategies" (implies free content is basic)
- "Secret methods" (implies withholding from free content)
- "Complete system" (free content should be complete)
- "Everything you need" (free content should have everything needed)
- "Premium content" (sounds elitist)
- "Exclusive access" (false scarcity)

✅ **Instead Say:**
- "Ready-made tools"
- "Time-saving templates"
- "Setup work done for you"
- "Implementation accelerator"
- "Convenience toolkit"

## Article Type Positioning

### How-To Guides

**Article Promise:**
"Complete step-by-step guide to [achieving X]. Everything you need to know in one place, with real examples and specific numbers."

**Playbook Add-On:**
"Want the spreadsheets and templates? Get the toolkit with everything pre-built for $[X]."

**Example:**
- **Article**: "How to Build a $1,000 Emergency Fund in 6 Months"
- **Playbook**: "Emergency Fund Toolkit: Spreadsheet, savings tracker, and automated setup guide"

### Listicles

**Article Promise:**
"[X] proven ways to [achieve goal]. Each method includes exactly how to do it, what to expect, and real examples."

**Playbook Add-On:**
"Get the action sheets, templates, and tracking tools for all [X] methods in one downloadable toolkit."

**Example:**
- **Article**: "15 Ways to Save $100 This Month"
- **Playbook**: "Money-Saving Toolkit: Calculators, trackers, and templates for all 15 methods"

### Comparison Guides

**Article Promise:**
"Complete comparison of [X vs Y vs Z]. Everything you need to make the right decision for your situation."

**Playbook Add-On:**
"Get the decision framework, comparison spreadsheet, and setup guides for your chosen option."

**Example:**
- **Article**: "Best Budgeting Methods: 50/30/20 vs Zero-Based vs Envelope System"
- **Playbook**: "Budgeting Setup Kit: Templates and trackers for all three methods"

### Tool Roundups

**Article Promise:**
"[X] best tools for [achieving goal]. Honest reviews, real pricing, and which one to choose for your situation."

**Playbook Add-On:**
"Get the setup guides, comparison spreadsheet, and optimization checklists for your chosen tools."

**Example:**
- **Article**: "Best Budgeting Apps for 2025"
- **Playbook**: "App Setup Toolkit: Configuration guides and tracking spreadsheets"

## Content Flow Strategy

### Article Structure for Maximum Value

1. **Hook (First 100 words)**
   - Specific problem/goal
   - Promise of complete solution
   - What makes this guide different

2. **Overview (Next 200 words)**
   - What you'll learn
   - Who this works for
   - What you'll need (usually nothing)

3. **Complete Strategy (Main content)**
   - Step-by-step instructions
   - Real examples with numbers
   - Common mistakes and how to avoid them

4. **Implementation Section**
   - Exactly how to get started
   - What to do first, second, third
   - How to track progress

5. **FAQ/Troubleshooting**
   - Common questions
   - What if scenarios
   - Additional tips

6. **Conclusion + Next Steps**
   - Recap key points
   - Clear action items
   - Natural bridge to playbook (if applicable)

### Playbook CTA Placement

**Natural Integration Points:**
- After explaining a complex setup process
- When mentioning "you'll need to create a spreadsheet"
- Before step-by-step implementation
- In FAQ when someone asks about templates
- At conclusion as natural next step

**CTA Integration Examples:**
- "To build this spreadsheet yourself, follow these steps... [steps] ...or grab the pre-built version in the [Name] Toolkit for $X."
- "You'll need to track several metrics for this to work. I've included a tracking sheet in the [Name] Playbook ($X) if you want to skip the setup."

## Pricing Psychology in Copy

### How to Present $5-$9 Price Points

**Comparison Anchors:**
- "Less than a coffee"
- "Cheaper than lunch"
- "About the cost of a magazine"
- "Less than most apps charge per month"

**Value Anchors:**
- "Save 3+ hours of setup time"
- "Skip the trial and error"
- "Get what took me [X hours] to build"
- "Avoid the common mistakes that cost time/money"

**Implementation Anchors:**
- "Start implementing today instead of next week"
- "Have everything ready in 15 minutes"
- "All tools in one download"
- "Nothing else to buy or figure out"

### Price Presentation Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- "$5" not "$5.00"
- "for $7" not "only $7"
- Bundle value: "5 tools for $9"
- Time value: "Save 3 hours for $5"

❌ **Don't:**
- Add false urgency
- Use "just" or "only" (sounds desperate)
- Compare to expensive courses
- Apologize for charging

## Quality Assurance Checklist

### For Every Article, Ensure:
- [ ] Complete strategy explained (no missing steps)
- [ ] Real examples with specific numbers
- [ ] Beginner-friendly language throughout
- [ ] Clear next steps at the end
- [ ] Value stands alone without playbook

### For Every Playbook Description, Ensure:
- [ ] Clear what tools are included
- [ ] Specific time savings mentioned
- [ ] No false scarcity or urgency
- [ ] Positioned as convenience, not necessity
- [ ] Price clearly stated with value anchor

### For Overall Positioning, Ensure:
- [ ] Free content delivers promised value
- [ ] Paid content enhances but doesn't replace
- [ ] Messaging appeals to all skill levels
- [ ] No fake social proof or testimonials
- [ ] Clear value ladder from free to paid

## Testing and Optimization

### A/B Test Ideas for Articles:
1. **Intro style**: Personal story vs direct value promise
2. **Complexity level**: Detailed steps vs simplified overview
3. **CTA placement**: Early vs mid vs end of article
4. **Audience targeting**: "Beginners" vs "Everyone" vs specific demographic

### A/B Test Ideas for Playbooks:
1. **Positioning**: "Time-saver" vs "Convenience" vs "Complete toolkit"
2. **Price presentation**: "$5" vs "for $5" vs "$5 (less than coffee)"
3. **Value proposition**: Feature-focused vs benefit-focused vs time-focused
4. **CTA copy**: "Get the toolkit" vs "Skip the setup" vs "Download now"

### Success Metrics:
- **Article engagement**: Time on page, scroll depth, completion rate
- **Playbook interest**: CTA clicks, conversion rate, completion rate
- **Overall value**: Email signups, return visits, customer satisfaction
- **Brand alignment**: Audience feedback, refund rates, support inquiries
