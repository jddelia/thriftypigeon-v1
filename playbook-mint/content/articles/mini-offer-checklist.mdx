Mini-offers win when they remove every ounce of friction between interest and purchase. Use this checklist when packaging your next $5-$9 playbook so buyers click before their coffee gets cold.

## Nail the transformation in one sentence

Write the promise like a progress bar. Example: "Go from messy receipts to tax-ready books in 48 hours." If you can’t explain the win quickly, the buyer can’t either.

## Anchor the value before you reveal the price

Start with the alternative. How much time, money, or energy would it cost to piece this together yourself? Stack three anchors (time saved, money earned, stress avoided) before you ever mention the $7 price tag.

## Give the CTA a speed advantage

If your checkout experience takes more than 30 seconds, you’re losing sales. Preload the product in your payment platform, enable Apple Pay/Google Pay, and auto-fill email when possible.

## Showcase instant proof

Drop screenshots, testimonials, or quick wins inside the article before the CTA. The goal is to have readers nodding along with "Yep, this is exactly what I need" moments.

## Make delivery delightful

Send the download instantly, include a bonus checklist, and give them one action they can take in the next 10 minutes. Momentum beats overwhelm.
