Most side hustles die in the idea graveyard. The ones that survive hit three checkpoints quickly: validation, pricing confidence, and delivery that doesn’t consume your life. These seven ideas pass all three tests.

## 1. Rentable Notion dashboards

Package the internal dashboards you already use for clients and productize them. Validation is one tweet or Reddit post away. Pricing anchor: $29-$49 for a template bundle.

## 2. Accountability sprints

Sell a 4-week cohort where you guide people through a transformation you already completed. Think “No-spend February” or “Pitch your freelance offer in 30 days.” Charge $99 with a money-back guarantee tied to showing up.

## 3. Local SEO tune-ups

Small businesses ignore Google Business Profiles. Offer a $249 package where you optimize listings, script review requests, and ship before/after reports.

## 4. Paid community office hours

If you already run a niche community, add a paid layer for weekly office hours. Price at $19/month with a cap so it stays high-touch.

## 5. Template swaps

Find an industry drowning in spreadsheets (wedding planners, fitness coaches, indie hackers). Swap their messy file with a clean template and charge $39 for lifetime access.

## 6. Loom teardown service

Offer 20-minute personalized audits recorded in Loom. Charge $79, deliver in 48 hours, and upsell a 1:1 call if they want more.

## 7. Rapid research reports

Aggregate the best insights on a trending topic, add your commentary, and sell a polished PDF. Think "AI for Etsy sellers" or "TikTok hooks that work in 2024." Price at $29 with an annual update option.

The key is to measure traction before overbuilding. Each of these ideas can be launched in a weekend using the frameworks inside the Emergency Fund Blueprint playbook.
